# MOBA Stylize Art

Production image library for the redesigned stylized MOBA concepts.

## Folder codes

- `Natural/tower` — Natural towers, mounted turret props, and projectiles.
- `Natural/resource` — bases, fences, mining structures, tools, and general props.
- `Natural/hero` — Natural hero sheets and hero equipment.
- `Natural/monster` — Natural monster sheets and monster equipment.
- `Galax/tower` — Galax towers, mounted weapons, and projectiles.
- `Galax/resource` — Galax bases, economy structures, tools, and general props.
- `Galax/hero` — Galax hero sheets and hero equipment.
- `Galax/monster` — Galax monster sheets and monster equipment.

## Filename pattern

`<faction>-<category><optional-number>-lv<level>.png`

- Factions: `nat`, `gal`
- Categories: `tw`, `res`, `hr`, `mn`
- First approved Natural tower sample: `Natural/tower/nat-tw-lv1.png`
- Additional examples: `nat-tw02-lv3.png`, `nat-mn01-lv1.png`, `gal-hr02-lv1.png`

Use lowercase ASCII names without spaces for all production images.

## Natural architecture rule

- Natural towers and buildings use refined organic high-fantasy architecture: pale carved stone, fitted warm hardwood, restrained bronze-gold, and one small crystal/rune accent.
- Front and back are both presentation façades and retain roughly 85% of the same silhouette, massing, outer pillars, arch/halo, and visual finish.
- The front uses the primary crest, entrance, or brighter core. The back uses a smaller secondary seal, closed arch, or dimmer crystal so direction remains readable.
- Avoid primitive rock piles, crude huts, tangled roots, uncontrolled overgrowth, broken ruins, and excessive filigree.
