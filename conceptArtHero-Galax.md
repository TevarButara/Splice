# Concept Art Prompts — HEROES · เผ่า GALAX ✦

> Galax = เอเลี่ยนเทคโนโลยีล้ำยุคจากดวงดาว: สะอาด เฉียบคม แม่นยำ และใช้ tech/utility ผ่าน shield, buff, gravity และ system hack
> งานทั้งหมดเป็น **premium stylized painterly fantasy MOBA concept art** ที่มีเทคโนโลยีต่างดาวเป็นองค์ประกอบ — heroic proportions, รูปทรงใหญ่สะอาด อ่านง่ายจากกล้อง top-down, production-ready และไม่เลียนแบบเกมหรือศิลปินที่มีอยู่

## Production workflow ต่อ Hero

- **A — Key Art:** ท่าโชว์ไดนามิก พร้อมอาวุธ/อุปกรณ์
- **B1 — Front + Back:** orthographic A-pose สำหรับ model/texture/rig
- **B2 — Left + Right:** orthographic A-pose profiles ใช้ Key Art+B1 เป็น references
- **P1/P2 — Combined lower-body equipment:** ถ้ากางเกง/ผ้าชาย/coat panels ต้องถอดจาก base body ให้รวมเป็น wearable set เดียว
- **C — Weapon / attached-device construction:** held weapon แยก prop; attached tech ทำ construction sheet แต่ติดอยู่ใน B1/B2
- **D — Skills + Icons:** Skill 1, Skill 2, Ultimate

## GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK

```text
Premium stylized PAINTERLY FANTASY MOBA game-hero concept art with restrained alien
technology and an original visual
identity. GALAX ARE TRUE EXTRATERRESTRIAL INTELLIGENT SPECIES, NOT EARTH HUMANS.
Their silhouettes may remain broadly bipedal for gameplay, but must have unmistakably
alien cranial structures, sensory systems, limb ratios, hands, feet and locomotion.
Use elongated or digitigrade legs, nonhuman shoulder/hip architecture, unusual torso
gaps, chitin/biometal transitions and species-specific proportions. NOT chibi, NOT
super-deformed, NOT mascot-like.

ABSOLUTELY AVOID: human face, human nose, human lips, human jaw, human ears, human
hairstyle, ordinary human eyes, colored-human skin, superhero bodysuit, Earth clothing
or a human wearing a sci-fi helmet. Heads should be evolved alien anatomy: faceted
cranial shells, sensory slits, compound eye clusters, transparent neural vessels,
mandible plates or bio-mechanical crests. Intelligence is conveyed through precision,
posture, tool use and advanced integrated technology—not through a human face.

HANDS / MANIPULATORS: each species uses a consistent intentional count of three to
five clearly separated articulated digits, including at least one opposing gripping
digit where it handles a weapon. No fused, duplicated, malformed or accidental digits.

RENDERING: polished 2.5D hand-painted game-art finish, broad sculpted planes,
controlled painterly-toon shadow groups, crisp silhouette and bold value grouping.
Use visible selective brush texture and graphic material cues instead of realistic
PBR response. Simplify every surface into a few large and medium shapes; keep only
focal small detail. Chitin, alien membrane, bio-metal, ceramic shell and contained
energy must feel magical and stylized rather than industrial or photorealistic. The
hero must read instantly during fast top-down MOBA gameplay and remain practical as
a clean mid-poly game model.

GALAX VISUAL LANGUAGE: technology has co-evolved with each species. Armor may be
grown chitin, cultured ceramic, living biometal or transparent neural glass; devices
connect into anatomical sockets instead of sitting on ordinary clothing. Use a small
number of clean geometric plates, broad quiet surfaces, intentional modules and energy contained INSIDE
lenses, blades, shields or body cores. Avoid generic modern military, NASA realism,
medieval armor with neon pasted on, human muscles wrapped in armor, cluttered wires,
random greebles, dense panel lines, tiny cables, realistic scratches, oversized
backpacks and excessive floating parts.

PRODUCTION DESIGN: preserve readable deformation or mechanical articulation at every
shoulder, elbow, wrist, torso, hip, knee, ankle and digit joint. Hard shells end before
joint creases; flexible membrane or telescoping biometal bridges the gap. Alien anatomy
may require a species-specific rig, but joint count and parenting must remain clear,
symmetrical where appropriate and feasible in Unity. Avoid fragile tendrils and dozens
of secondary appendages unless they are a small number of clearly riggable pieces.

COLOR SYSTEM: each Galax hero may use a different dominant palette. Preserve faction
identity through construction language rather than one shared color. Neon accents are
focused and limited; do not cover the entire character in emissive material.

No aura, no energy swirls and no floating particles around the character. Magic/tech
shows only inside attached devices, eyes, weapon edges, shield lenses or embedded cores.
Clean pure white background, full body fully visible, both hands and feet inside canvas,
no cropped limbs, no extreme perspective, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.
```

## B1 — FRONT + BACK BLOCK

```text
Create SHEET B1 only: exactly TWO large full-body orthographic views in one horizontal
row, exact FRONT and exact BACK. Identical scale/proportions, 80–85% canvas height,
generous white space and no overlap. Strict neutral A-pose: both arms straight and
lowered diagonally about 35–45 degrees from the shoulders, palms angled toward the
thighs while leaving a clear air gap between hands and hips. Legs straight and slightly
apart. Show all joint zones, major seams, armor thickness,
device roots and back construction. Remove held weapons and all detachable equipment.
Keep only permanent anatomical tech.
Hands/manipulators are open and preserve the approved species-specific digit count,
with every digit separated and articulated. Pure white background, no side views, no text.
```

## B2 — LEFT + RIGHT BLOCK

```text
Create SHEET B2 only: exactly TWO large orthographic profiles in one horizontal row,
exact LEFT facing left and exact RIGHT facing right. Match approved Key Art and B1
exactly in identity, proportions, panel count, colors, textures and attachments. Show
side thickness, layer order and silhouette depth. Strict anatomical A-pose in 3D: arms
extend sideways and downward about 35–45 degrees in the coronal plane. In a true side
orthographic view the near and far arms therefore appear largely overlapped/end-on,
with the hands finishing below and outside the hip silhouette. NEVER rotate or sweep
the arms forward/backward merely to show their length. If inspection requires it,
offset the far arm vertically by no more than 2–3% while preserving the same A-pose
axis. Remove held weapons and all detachable
equipment; keep only permanent anatomical tech. Pure white background, no extra views,
no text, no perspective distortion.
```

# Hero 1 — Veyra, the Comet Lancer (ไฟท์เตอร์หอกดาวหาง) ☄️

> Comet-lancer caste Fighter/Initiator — นักรบต่างดาวสรีระ digitigrade หัวเป็น cranial blade ไม่มีใบหน้ามนุษย์ ใช้ momentum organ ขับหอกยาว

## A — Key Art
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
The hero is "Veyra the Comet Lancer", an adult comet-lancer caste alien Fighter /
Initiator. Her silhouette is tall, predatory and unmistakably extraterrestrial: narrow
floating-rib torso with a visible gap beneath the chest carapace, elongated double-
jointed arms, reverse-jointed digitigrade legs, blade-like heel spurs and a forward-
leaning center of mass. No human anatomy beneath a suit.

HEAD: no human face, hair or ears. A smooth swept-back blade-shaped cranial shell with
exactly THREE narrow pale-cyan sensory slits arranged in a vertical triangular cluster.
Two small lateral vibration fins sit behind the shell. The mouth is a sealed vent line
under the chin plate; no lips, nose or human jaw.

BODY / TECH: charcoal living-biometal skeleton, exposed coral-red tensile bio-fibers at
upper arms and thighs, and asymmetrical porcelain cultured-ceramic shell plates grown
directly into anatomical sockets. Burnt comet-orange marks the momentum channels;
pale cyan is limited to sensor slits. A compact momentum organ sits between shoulder
blades with exactly THREE swept-back porcelain comet fins and an orange glass core.
No clothing, belt, human under-suit or hair.

MANIPULATORS: each hand has exactly FOUR long articulated alien digits—three forward
gripping digits and one opposing inner digit—with dark chitin tips. Preserve this count.

WEAPON: a separate two-handed COMET LANCE, approximately 1.3 times her body height,
with a slim dark-metal shaft, broad asymmetric porcelain spearhead, contained orange
plasma channel and cyan targeting lens. Both four-digit manipulators grip it clearly.

Pose: explosive diagonal lunge with the lance sweeping forward, but retain readable
anatomy and avoid extreme foreshortening. Full body and entire lance inside canvas.
```

## B — Turnaround identity
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
Character turnaround of the SAME true extraterrestrial Veyra from approved Key Art:
blade-shaped faceless cranial shell with exactly three cyan sensory slits, narrow
floating-rib torso, elongated double-jointed arms, four-digit manipulators, digitigrade
legs, charcoal living biometal, coral tensile fibers, porcelain cultured shell,
comet-orange channels, compact back momentum organ and exactly three swept-back fins.
No human face, hair, ears, clothing or Earth footwear. Remove the held lance; retain
the attached momentum organ. Append exactly one block: B1 Front+Back or B2 Left+Right.
```

## C — Weapon prop
```text
Isolated COMET LANCE of Veyra, no character: slim dark-metal shaft, asymmetric
porcelain spearhead, contained orange plasma channel, cyan targeting lens and compact
counterweight. Exactly three orthographic views: front, side, back. White background.
```

# Hero 2 — Orryx Aegis-9 (แทงค์เมคโล่สนามแรง) 🛡️

> Crustacean guardian bio-mech Tank — กระดองกว้าง หัวเป็นกลุ่ม sensory pits ไม่มีหน้ามนุษย์ ใช้ shield projector แขนซ้ายและ gravity maul

## A — Key Art
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
"Orryx Aegis-9", a sentient crustacean guardian bio-mech Tank, not a human-shaped
robot. Huge low dome carapace spanning both shoulders, compressed wedge torso, recessed
neck and six cyan sensory pits set into a small beakless head under the shell. Two
massive main arms, broad three-toed digitigrade feet and exactly four thick mechanical
digits per hand. Bone-ivory cultured shell, navy contractile tissue, dark biometal joints,
cyan shield cells and sparse safety-yellow markings. LEFT hand equips a separate
removable hexagonal shield projector; right hand holds a separate gravity maul. Both
items are detached for body turnaround sheets and receive independent prop sheets.
No human face, helmet or proportions.
```

## B — Turnaround identity
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
Character turnaround of the SAME Orryx from approved Key Art: huge low dome carapace,
compressed wedge torso, recessed beakless head with six cyan sensory pits, massive arms,
four-digit hands, broad three-toed digitigrade feet, bone-ivory shell, navy tissue and
dark biometal joints. REMOVE both the detachable shield projector and gravity maul.
Body-only strict A-pose. Append exactly one block: B1 Front+Back or B2 Left+Right.
```

## C — Separate props
```text
Create independent orthographic prop sheets for: (1) detachable HEXAGONAL SHIELD
PROJECTOR and (2) GRAVITY MAUL. Never attach either item to Orryx's body turnaround.
Exactly three views per prop: front, side, back. Pure white background, no character.
```

## D — Skills
- **Aegis Wall:** project a wide directional barrier.
- **Mass Lock:** increase local gravity, slowing enemies and preventing knockback.
- **Ultimate — Citadel Mode:** anchor in place, enlarge shield coverage and protect allies.

# Hero 3 — Ilyon, the Starshot (มาร์กสแมนปืนอนุภาค) ✧

> Long-limbed rail-seer Marksman — หัวทรงเสา sensory visor เดียว ขายาวแบบ stilt และแขนเล็งปืนยาวผิดสัดส่วนมนุษย์

## A — Key Art
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
"Ilyon the Starshot", a powerful heavy rail-seer alien Marksman. Broad chest cage,
massive shoulders, dense forearms and thighs, long digitigrade legs and three-pronged feet.
The body is approximately 35–45% thicker than a slender rail-seer while retaining long
nonhuman limb ratios. Head is a tall dark
vertical sensory column with one recessed acid-yellow optical slit; no face, eyes,
mouth, ears or hair. Graphite chitin and oxidized-olive flexible tissue are integrated

digit per hand. Separate enormous two-handed sci-fi particle cannon, approximately 80%
of his body height: thick wedge silhouette, dominant pearl-white ceramic armor shells,
deep crimson-red metal chassis, dark graphite joints, contained red-orange reactor
chambers, small gold trim and sparse turquoise targeting nodes. Calm precise firing
pose; clearly not a human in armor.
```

## D — Skills

# Hero 4 — Serapha Eventide (เมจควบคุมแรงโน้มถ่วง) ◉

> Monolith energy-oracle Mage/Controller — เอเลี่ยนทรงสูงไหล่กว้าง หัวเป็น obsidian prism ไร้ใบหน้า ดีไซน์เรียบ ควบคุมพลังงานม่วงอัดแน่น

## A — Key Art
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
"Serapha Eventide", a monolith energy-oracle alien Mage/Controller with a deliberately
clean design and approximately 70% fewer separate shapes than an ornate sci-fi mage.
Exactly TWO arms. Tall alien stature, broad heavy shoulder carapaces, narrow vertical
torso, long reverse-jointed legs and split two-toed feet. Head is a small faceless
elongated OBSIDIAN PRISM suspended inside one simple open ivory cranial yoke, with one
thin vertical violet sensory slit; no human face, skull, hair, eyes or mouth. Each hand
has exactly FOUR separated articulated digits.

Use five dominant shape groups only: head/yoke, shoulder carapace, central torso,
forearms and legs. Smooth matte charcoal and deep blue-black cultured-ceramic plates,
muted pearl-grey shoulder edges, sparse dark-crimson structural accents, broad quiet
surfaces and very few seams. One recessed vertical violet sternum core with only two
short energy channels. NO back crown, ring, halo, wings or repeated glowing gems.

Exactly THREE rigid vertical lower-torso armor vanes end above the knees with full leg
clearance; they are solid mechanical plates, never fabric. Separate simple tall
asymmetric staff: straight dark shaft and compact angular head holding one caged violet
crystal. Left forearm emitter grows one dense solid violet energy shard. Energy appears
only in the sensory slit, sternum core, staff crystal and forearm shard. NO cloth, robe,
dress, skirt, cape, banner, smoke, aura, floating particles or detached components.
```

## D — Skills
- **Gravity Well:** place a compact field that pulls enemies inward.
- **Eventide Seal:** suspend one target briefly and reduce its attack speed.
- **Ultimate — Monolith Collapse:** overload the sternum core and staff crystal to create a large controlled gravity-collapse zone.

# Hero 5 — Tovan, the Halo Smith (ซัพพอร์ตวิศวกรโล่) ⊕

## A — Key Art
```text
[GALAX HERO STYLE BLOCK]
"Tovan the Halo Smith", a forge-symbiote alien Support/Engineer. No human face or hair.
Small pearl-chitin head shaped as a radial six-petal sensor crown surrounding a dark
central mint lens; lower head ends in three closed communication plates. Slender torso,
long double-elbow arms, wide stable pelvis and four-toed feet. Pearl chitin, flexible
grey membrane and champagne-bronze living conduits. Circular mint halo organs are grown
into both forearms; flat repair core fused to upper back. Four articulated digits per
hand. Separate tool staff. Intelligent precise posture, no Earth clothing or human body.
```

## D — Skills

- **Overclock Link:** connect to one ally, granting attack and movement speed.

# Hero 6 — Khex, the Riftstalker (แอสซาซินใบมีดเฟส) ⟡

> Rift-mantis Assassin — หัวแมลงรูป wedge, ขา digitigrade สูง และแขนใบมีดเฟสแบบชีวกล

## A — Key Art
```text

"Khex the Riftstalker", a rift-mantis alien Assassin. Narrow triangular thorax, very
```

## D — Skills
